1DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Customer" or "User"

Means an individual who accesses or uses the CloudCity Food Mini App or submits an Order or service request through the Mini App.

1.2 "Merchant"

Means a restaurant, food outlet, shop, service provider, or other business offering products or services through the Mini App.

1.3 "Rider" or "Driver"

Means a person assigned through the CloudCity platform to provide Food Delivery, Deli, Say Buy, or Taxi Service.

1.4 "Order"

Means a request submitted by a Customer through the Mini App for products or services.

1.5 "Mini App"

Means the CloudCity Food Mini App operating inside the KBZPay application.

1.6 "Food Delivery"

Means the ordering and delivery of food or other products from participating Merchants.

1.7 "Deli"

Means a delivery service through which a Rider collects and delivers permitted items between locations specified by the Customer.

1.8 "Say Buy"

Means a service through which a Rider purchases permitted products on behalf of the Customer and delivers them to the Customer.

1.9 "Taxi Service"

Means a ride-booking service that connects Customers with available Drivers.

1.10 "Store Pickup"

Means a service option under which the Customer collects an Order directly from the Merchant's location.

1.11 "Mobile Top-up"

Means the purchase of supported mobile phone credit through CloudCity Balance or another supported payment method displayed in the Mini App.

1.12 "CloudCity Balance"

Means the digital balance recorded in the Customer's CloudCity account and available for supported services.

1.13 "KBZPay"

Means the digital payment services provided by Kanbawza Bank Limited through the KBZPay application.

1.14 "Refund"

Means a full or partial repayment approved in connection with a cancelled, unavailable, incorrect, damaged, incomplete, duplicated, or otherwise unsatisfactory Order or transaction.

1.15 "Business Day"

Means a day other than Saturday, Sunday, a public holiday, or a bank holiday in Myanmar.

15CANCELLATION AND REFUNDS

15.1 Cancellation Before Merchant Acceptance

The Customer may request cancellation before the Merchant accepts a Food Delivery Order.

Where payment has already been completed, an approved Refund will be processed to the Customer's original KBZPay account.

15.2 Cancellation After Merchant Acceptance

After the Merchant accepts the Order, cancellation and Refund eligibility will depend on:

  • the Merchant's preparation progress;
  • whether the product has been prepared;
  • whether a Rider has been assigned;
  • whether the Rider has started travelling;
  • the reason for cancellation;
  • the condition of the product;
  • costs already incurred; and
  • CloudCity's applicable cancellation and Refund policy.

A full Refund is not guaranteed after the Merchant begins preparation.

15.3 Merchant Cancellation

Where the Merchant cannot fulfil the Order, CloudCity may cancel the Order and arrange a full or partial Refund, as appropriate.

15.4 Partial Refund

A partial Refund may be processed where:

  • one or more items are unavailable;
  • an item is missing;
  • an incorrect item is delivered;
  • an item is damaged or spilled;
  • the quantity is incorrect; or
  • another partial Refund is approved by CloudCity.

15.5 Refund Method

For Orders paid using KBZPay through the CloudCity Food Mini App, all approved full or partial Refunds will be processed automatically to the Customer's original KBZPay account through the KBZPay Refund API.

15.6 Refund Processing Time

Refund processing and crediting time may vary depending on:

  • KBZPay system processing;
  • technical conditions;
  • reconciliation;
  • compliance review;
  • banking procedures; and
  • other relevant operational processes.

15.7 Duplicate Payment

Where a duplicate payment is confirmed, CloudCity will investigate the transaction and, where approved, process the duplicate amount through the applicable Refund method.

15.8 Deli, Say Buy, and Taxi Cancellation

Cancellation fees, waiting fees, or unsuccessful-trip fees may apply where a Rider or Driver has already accepted, started travelling, arrived, purchased goods, or incurred reasonable expenses.
