🛵 Cloud City Rider Cooperation Agreement

Effective Date: January 27, 2026 | Applies to all riders cooperating with Cloud City

1. Introduction

Welcome to the Cloud City rider team. This agreement sets out the cooperation relationship, rights, and obligations between riders and the Cloud City platform.

🤝 Cooperation Statement

Cloud City and riders have a free cooperation relationship, not an employment relationship. The platform does not mandate working hours — riders are free to choose when to go online and accept orders. The platform uses incentive-based encouragement.

2. Eligibility Requirements

  • Age: 18 to 60 years old
  • National ID: Valid Myanmar national identity card (copy)
  • Driver's License: Valid motorcycle or bicycle license (if applicable)
  • Home Address Proof: Copy of household registration or address document
  • Area Residency Proof: Work permit or residency proof confirming residence in the delivery area

3. Review Process

  • Applications will be reviewed within 3 working days
  • Upon approval, the business team will contact the rider to complete onboarding
  • Onboarding is completely free

4. Vehicle Requirements

  • Both motorcycles and bicycles are accepted for delivery
  • Vehicles must be in good working condition to ensure safe delivery
  • Riders must comply with Myanmar traffic laws and drive safely
  • The platform is not liable for vehicle damage or accidents

5. Order Rules

Free Order Acceptance:

  • Riders may freely choose when to go online — no mandatory hours
  • Riders may freely accept or decline orders

Rejection Limits:

  • A maximum of 2 rejections per every 10 orders is allowed (rejection rate ≤ 20%)
  • Exceeding this limit will result in reduced order allocation from the system
  • Persistently high rejection rates will affect the rider's score and settlement cycle

6. Delivery Standards

  • Riders must arrive at the merchant promptly after accepting an order
  • Maintain good communication with users and merchants throughout delivery
  • Treat users and merchants with courtesy and respect — harassment or misconduct is strictly prohibited
  • Handle food and packages with care to ensure safe delivery
  • Do not open or tamper with packages or food items
  • Do not use the delivery service to transport contraband prohibited by Myanmar law

7. Delivery Fee & Settlement

Delivery Fee:

  • Delivery fees are calculated according to the platform's commission rules and credited to the rider's platform balance

Settlement Cycle (automatically adjusted based on rider score):

  • New riders: Settlement every 15 days
  • Good-rated riders: Automatically upgraded to every 7 days

Settlement Method: KBZPay

8. Rating System

  • Ratings are based on on-time delivery rate, user reviews, rejection rate, and complaint records
  • Higher-rated riders receive more orders and faster settlement cycles
  • Persistently low ratings will result in reduced order allocation or account suspension
📋 Rating Details

Specific scoring criteria are subject to platform announcements and may be updated from time to time.

9. Violations & Penalties

  • Late delivery: Score deduction, affects settlement cycle
  • Frequent complaints: Score deduction, possible financial penalty
  • Poor attitude: Score deduction, possible penalty or account suspension
  • Harassing users or merchants: Immediate account suspension
  • Transporting contraband: Immediate account suspension and referral to authorities

Specific penalty amounts are subject to platform announcements and may be adjusted.

10. Insurance

  • The platform does not provide accident insurance for riders
  • Riders are encouraged to purchase personal accident or third-party liability insurance
  • Riders are solely responsible for any accidents or injuries occurring during delivery

11. Nature of Cooperation

  • Riders and Cloud City have an independent cooperation relationship, not an employment relationship
  • The platform does not mandate working hours or locations
  • Riders independently decide when and where to work
  • This agreement does not constitute a labor contract of any kind

12. Termination

  • Riders may stop cooperating with the platform at any time without prior notice
  • All accepted orders must be completed before stopping
  • Outstanding balance will be paid according to the normal settlement cycle

13. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

  • This agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of the Union of Myanmar
  • Disputes shall first be resolved through friendly negotiation
  • If negotiation fails, disputes shall be submitted to the competent court of Mandalay Region
  • In the event of conflict between language versions, the English version shall prevail
