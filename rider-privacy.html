CloudCityFood Rider Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 27 January 2026 | Applies to: Delivery riders on the CloudCityFood platform

Introduction

CloudCityFood respects and values the privacy of its delivery Riders. This CloudCityFood Rider Privacy Policy (“Policy”) applies to the collection, use, storage, sharing, and protection of your Personal Data when you register an account, accept Orders, collect items, make deliveries, receive delivery earnings, or use other related functions through the CloudCityFood Rider application, Rider webpages, or other Rider service channels.

This Policy also explains:

  • the categories of Rider information we collect;
  • why we need to process such information;
  • the circumstances in which we may share such information;
  • how long such information may be retained;
  • how you may access, correct, delete, or control your information; and
  • how you may submit a privacy complaint or exercise your other rights.
Important Notice: Due to the nature of on-demand delivery services, the Rider App must use a Rider’s real-time location for Order assignment, navigation, delivery tracking, mileage calculation, and safety management. When you are “Accepting Orders” or actively performing a delivery, the Rider App will collect your precise location information. To maintain real-time delivery functions, location collection may continue in the background even when the Rider App is not displayed in the foreground or when your device screen is locked. Please carefully review Section 3.3, “Location Information,” before using the Rider Services.

Before registering for or using the Rider Services, please carefully read and understand this Policy. Where separate consent is required under applicable requirements or device permission rules, we will provide an appropriate permission request, prominent disclosure, or other notice and request your authorization.

Commitment Not to Sell Personal Data

CloudCityFood does not sell or rent your Personal Data and will not transfer your Personal Data to unrelated third parties in exchange for money or other commercial benefit.

Without your express consent, we will not provide Personal Data that directly identifies you to unrelated third parties for their own independent advertising or marketing purposes.

This Section does not prevent CloudCityFood from processing or sharing information where reasonably necessary to:

  • verify a Rider’s identity and service eligibility;
  • assign and manage delivery Orders;
  • provide necessary delivery information to Customers and Merchants;
  • provide mapping, location, navigation, and real-time delivery tracking;
  • calculate delivery distance and Rider earnings;
  • process earnings settlements through banks or KBZPay;
  • provide customer support and handle complaints;
  • prevent fraud, investigate incidents, or resolve delivery disputes; or
  • comply with applicable legal, tax, accounting, audit, regulatory, or judicial obligations.

Information We Collect

CloudCityFood collects Rider information only to the extent reasonably necessary to register Riders, provide delivery services, protect transactions, settle earnings, handle complaints, and fulfil applicable obligations.

3.1 Information You Provide

When you register a Rider Account, undergo eligibility verification, or use the Rider Services, you may be required to provide:

  • your name, telephone number, and email address;
  • identity documents, identity-verification information, and identification numbers;
  • a driving licence, driving qualification, or other qualification required for delivery services;
  • your photograph, profile picture, or identity-verification photograph;
  • vehicle type, vehicle registration number, vehicle photographs, and other vehicle information;
  • information concerning your delivery box, helmet, uniform, or other required equipment;
  • bank account, KBZPay account, or other payment account information used to receive Rider earnings;
  • emergency-contact information or other contact details reasonably required for safety management;
  • information you provide when accepting Orders, responding to Order issues, replying to ratings, reporting incidents, submitting complaints or appeals, or communicating with Customer Support; and
  • Order photographs, collection evidence, proof of delivery, incident records, or other documents you voluntarily upload.

You must ensure that the information you provide is true, accurate, complete, valid, and relates to you. Where you provide another person’s information, you must ensure that you have obtained appropriate authorization from that person.

You must promptly update or notify CloudCityFood if your identity, vehicle, driving qualification, or payment information changes.

3.2 Information Automatically Collected When You Use the Rider Services

When you log in to or use the Rider Services, we may automatically collect:

  • your IP address, device model, device brand, operating system, and system version;
  • the Rider App version, language, time zone, and network status;
  • device identifiers, application-instance information, and necessary security identifiers;
  • login time, logout time, online status, and Order-acceptance status;
  • operation logs, error logs, crash logs, and security logs;
  • records of Orders accepted, rejected, cancelled, collected, delivered, and completed;
  • delivery routes, delivery distances, travelled mileage, and delivery completion times;
  • arrival time at the Merchant, waiting time, collection time, delivery time, and Order-processing time;
  • Order acceptance rate, completion rate, cancellation rate, ratings, complaints, and violation records;
  • delivery earnings, incentives, subsidies, deductions, adjustments, and settlement status; and
  • information generated through cookies, local storage, software development kits, or similar technologies.

Device information, log information, and delivery data may constitute Personal Data where they are linked to your Account, telephone number, identity information, vehicle details, or Order records. Such information should not automatically be treated as anonymous information.

3.3 Location Information

3.3.1 Important Notice: Background Collection of Precise Location Information

To provide essential Rider functions, including intelligent Order assignment, map navigation, real-time delivery tracking, delivery-distance calculation, earnings settlement, safety management, and incident handling, the CloudCityFood Rider App collects your precise location information through GPS, Wi-Fi, mobile networks, and device-location services.

The Rider App may continuously collect your precise location when any of the following applies:

  • you are logged in to your Rider Account and your status is set to “Accepting Orders,” “Online,” or another status under which you may receive Orders;
  • you have accepted an Order and are travelling to the Merchant or collection point;
  • you are delivering goods or an Order to a Customer; or
  • the Order has not yet been completed and real-time location information remains necessary for delivery tracking, dispatch, or safety management.

To maintain real-time dispatch and delivery tracking, location collection may continue in the background even when the Rider App is not visible in the foreground, when you are using another application, or when your device screen is locked.

3.3.2 When Background Location Collection Starts and Stops

The Rider App will generally collect precise background location information only when:

  • you are logged in to your Rider Account;
  • you are available to accept Orders; or
  • you have an active delivery Order.

When you have completed all active Orders and change your status to “On Break,” “Stop Accepting Orders,” “Offline,” or another status clearly indicating that you are no longer available to receive Orders, the Rider App should stop collecting background location information for dispatch and delivery purposes.

When you log out of your Rider Account, the Rider App should stop collecting background location information associated with Rider dispatch and delivery services.

There may be a brief delay in stopping location collection due to system synchronization delays, an Order status that has not yet been updated, an unexpected application closure, or device operating-system restrictions. CloudCityFood will take reasonable measures to minimize unnecessary location collection.

3.3.3 Purposes of Processing Location Information

We may use your location information and delivery-route records for the following purposes:

  • Intelligent Order Assignment: To match you with nearby Orders based on your real-time location, availability, and existing Order status.
  • Order Dispatch: To determine the distance between the Rider, Merchant, collection point, and delivery address.
  • Real-Time Delivery Tracking: To display delivery progress and the Rider’s approximate real-time location to Platform dispatch personnel and the relevant Customer.
  • Navigation Assistance: To provide directions to Merchants, collection points, and Customer delivery addresses.
  • Mileage Calculation: To calculate delivery distance, actual travel distance, or other relevant chargeable mileage.
  • Earnings Settlement: To calculate or verify delivery fees, incentives, subsidies, and other Rider earnings.
  • Operational Management: To analyse delivery efficiency, waiting time, Order completion, and service-area operations.
  • Safety Protection: To assist in verifying a Rider’s location in the event of loss of contact, a traffic accident, unusual stopping, or another emergency.
  • Complaint and Dispute Handling: To verify necessary trip and route information in cases involving non-delivery, incorrect delivery, loss of goods, delivery abnormalities, or payment disputes.
  • Fraud Prevention: To identify false delivery completion, abnormal mileage, irregular Order acceptance, or other conduct that may affect the rights and interests of CloudCityFood, Customers, Merchants, or Riders.

3.3.4 Display and Sharing of Location Information

During the delivery of an Order:

  • the Customer may view your approximate real-time location and delivery progress;
  • the Merchant may receive necessary status or location information relating to your arrival for collection;
  • authorized CloudCityFood operations, dispatch, customer-support, safety, or finance personnel may access relevant location and route records where necessary to perform their duties; and
  • mapping, navigation, location, or cloud-service providers may process necessary location information within the scope of services entrusted to them.

Except where necessary to complete a delivery, protect safety, resolve a dispute, comply with applicable obligations, or fulfil another purpose expressly stated in this Policy, CloudCityFood will not sell or arbitrarily disclose your precise location or delivery-route records to unrelated third parties.

3.3.5 Your Control Over Location Permissions

You may review or disable the Rider App’s location permissions through your device settings.

However, precise location and background location are essential permissions for Order acceptance, dispatch, navigation, and real-time delivery tracking. If you disable, reject, or restrict location permissions:

  • you may be unable to switch to an Order-acceptance status;
  • you may be unable to receive nearby Orders;
  • the Platform may be unable to verify your arrival at a Merchant or delivery destination;
  • navigation, mileage calculation, and delivery-earnings calculation may not function properly; and
  • you may be unable to continue performing an active delivery Order.

Disabling location permissions will not automatically delete location or route records that were previously collected in accordance with this Policy or applicable requirements. You may request Account deletion or submit an applicable data request under Sections 12 and 14 of this Policy.

Basis for Processing Information

We may process your information on one or more of the following grounds:

  • to perform the Rider service arrangement between you and CloudCityFood, including Account verification, Order assignment, delivery, navigation, tracking, settlement, and customer support;

How We Share Information

CloudCityFood shares Rider information only where reasonably necessary in connection with the Rider Services.

6.1 Sharing with Customers

To complete a delivery Order, we may display or provide the relevant Customer with:

  • the Rider’s name, nickname, or Rider identifier;
  • the Rider’s profile photograph;
  • the Rider’s approximate real-time location during delivery;
  • delivery progress and estimated arrival information;
  • a telephone number or in-Platform contact function necessary to complete the Order; and
  • vehicle type or other information reasonably necessary to identify the assigned Rider.

Customers may use such information only to receive and confirm the relevant Order and must not use Rider information for harassment, marketing, tracking, or any unrelated purpose.

6.2 Sharing with Merchants

To complete collection and handover of an Order, we may provide the relevant Merchant with:

  • the Rider’s name, nickname, or Rider identifier;
  • the Rider’s telephone number or an in-Platform contact function;
  • vehicle or Order-identification information;
  • arrival status and estimated collection time; and
  • information relating to unavailable items, incorrect products, cancellation, refunds, or other Order issues.

Merchants may use such information only to prepare, hand over, and handle the relevant Order.

6.3 Sharing with Service Providers

We may share necessary information with service providers acting on our behalf, including:

  • mapping, location, and navigation providers;
  • KBZPay, banks, and other settlement providers;
  • cloud-hosting, server, and data-storage providers;

  • customer-support, technical-support, and data-analysis providers;

  • insurance, incident-response, or emergency-assistance providers.

Such service providers may process information only within the scope authorized and entrusted by CloudCityFood and may not use the information for their own independent and unrelated purposes.

6.4 Legal and Safety Requirements

We may retain, use, or disclose necessary information, including location and trip records, where:

  • requested by a government authority, court, or regulatory body with lawful authority;
  • necessary to investigate suspected fraud, theft, unlawful conduct, or unauthorized Account use;
  • necessary to handle traffic accidents, personal-safety incidents, or delivery accidents;
  • necessary to investigate non-delivery, false delivery completion, loss of goods, or food-safety issues;
  • necessary to protect the personal or property safety of Customers, Merchants, Riders, employees, or the public;
  • necessary to establish, exercise, or defend the lawful rights of CloudCityFood or another relevant party; or
  • necessary to handle complaints, insurance claims, arbitration, litigation, or other dispute proceedings.

6.5 Corporate Transactions

If CloudCityFood undergoes a merger, acquisition, reorganization, financing, asset transfer, or business succession, relevant Rider information may be transferred to transaction participants or a successor entity to the extent reasonably necessary.

We will require the receiving party to continue protecting the information to a standard no lower than that set out in this Policy. Where such a change may materially affect Rider rights, we will provide notice through reasonable means.

  • restrict the purposes and scope for which the recipient may process the information;
  • require the recipient to comply with confidentiality and security obligations;
  • restrict unauthorized access, use, and onward disclosure; and
  • apply security measures appropriate to the nature of the information.

Where separate consent is required for relevant cross-border processing, we will provide an appropriate notice and request your consent.

Information Security

CloudCityFood will implement reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the nature, sensitivity, and processing risks of Rider information to protect it against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, loss, or destruction.

Such measures may include:

  • security measures for data transmission and storage;
  • Account authentication and access controls;
  • restrictions on employee and service-provider access;
  • access logs, operational logs, and security monitoring;
  • data backup and recovery measures;
  • device, Account, and abnormal-login detection; and
  • security-incident investigation and response procedures.

Precise location information and delivery-route records may significantly affect Rider rights and interests. CloudCityFood will restrict access to such information and retain it only for as long as necessary for Order assignment, delivery tracking, earnings settlement, safety management, incident investigation, dispute handling, or compliance with relevant obligations.

No method of internet transmission or electronic storage can guarantee absolute security. If an information-security incident may materially affect Rider rights, we will take reasonable containment and remedial measures based on the nature of the incident and, where appropriate, notify affected Riders.

Data-Retention Periods

We retain Rider information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Policy.

Our general retention principles are as follows:

  • Account and Identity Information: Retained while the Rider Account remains active and for as long as necessary for eligibility verification and provision of services.
  • Driving and Vehicle Information: Retained for as long as necessary to verify eligibility, handle safety matters, and comply with applicable obligations.
  • Order and Delivery Records: Retained for the periods required for tax, accounting, audit, regulatory, and dispute-resolution purposes.
  • Location and Delivery-Route Records: Retained only for as long as necessary for Order assignment, delivery tracking, mileage calculation, earnings settlement, safety management, and dispute handling, after which they will be deleted or anonymized.
  • Earnings and Settlement Records: Retained for as long as necessary to complete payment, reconciliation, tax, audit, and dispute resolution.
  • Complaint, Incident, and Customer-Support Records: Retained for as long as necessary to complete investigations, handle appeals, and prevent repeated disputes.
  • Log and Security Records: Retained for a reasonable period based on security risks, technical requirements, and internal retention rules.
  • Marketing-Consent Records: After consent is withdrawn, records may be retained where necessary to demonstrate the original consent and subsequent withdrawal.

After the applicable retention period expires, we will delete, anonymize, or otherwise appropriately dispose of the information, unless continued retention is required for legal, tax, accounting, audit, regulatory, judicial, insurance, or dispute-resolution purposes.

Account Deletion and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your Rider Account through the Account-deletion function in the Rider App or by contacting CloudCityFood Customer Support.

After receiving a valid request, we will, within a reasonable period and generally within ninety (90) days, delete or anonymize Rider Account information that is no longer required.

The following information may not be deleted immediately after Account deletion:

  • Order and settlement records that must be retained for tax, accounting, audit, or regulatory purposes;
  • information required to complete outstanding earnings, refunds, deduction reviews, or financial reconciliation;
  • information required to handle incomplete Orders, complaints, appeals, incidents, or insurance matters;
  • information reasonably necessary to prevent fraud, investigate unlawful conduct, protect safety, or resolve disputes; and
  • other information required to be retained under applicable obligations.

Account deletion is final and cannot be reversed. Before requesting deletion, you must complete any active Orders and properly resolve outstanding earnings, appeals, incidents, and other unfinished matters.

Account deletion will not automatically release you from any Order, settlement, complaint, incident, violation, compensation, or other obligation or liability that arose before deletion.

A person under eighteen years of age may not register a Rider Account, accept delivery Orders, or provide services as a CloudCityFood Rider.

If we discover that a minor has registered or used a Rider Account, we may immediately restrict, suspend, or close the relevant Account and may delete or restrict the use of the related information as appropriate.

  • အကောင့်ဝင်ရောက်မှု အခြေအနေကို ထိန်းသိမ်းထားရန်၊ ဘာသာစကား၊ ဒေသ နှင့် အခြား ဆက်တင်များကို မှတ်သားထားရန်၊ အကောင့် နှင့် စနစ် လုံခြုံရေးကို ထိန်းသိမ်းရန်၊ လုပ်ဆောင်ချက် သုံးစွဲမှု အခြေအနေများကို ဆန်းစစ်ရန်၊ နည်းပညာ အမှားအယွင်းများကို ခွဲခြားသတ်မှတ်ရန်၊ အက်ပလီကေးရှင်း နှင့် စာမျက်နှာ စွမ်းဆောင်ရည်ကို တိုးတက်စေရန်၊ မသမာမှု နှင့် ပုံမှန်မဟုတ်သော လုပ်ဆောင်ချက်များကို တားဆီးရန်။ သင်သည် စက်ပစ္စည်း သို့မဟုတ် ဘရောက်ဆာ ဆက်တင်များမှတစ်ဆင့် သက်ဆိုင်ရာ နည်းပညာများကို စီမံခန့်ခွဲနိုင်ပါသည်။ သို့သော် လိုအပ်သော လုပ်ဆောင်ချက်များကို ပိတ်ပင်ခြင်းသည် ပို့ဆောင်သူသုံး အချို့သော ဝန်ဆောင်မှုများကို ပုံမှန် အသုံးပြုနိုင်ခြင်း မရှိအောင် ဖြစ်ပေါ်စေနိုင်ပါသည်

ဒေတာ သိမ်းဆည်းခြင်း နှင့် နယ်စပ်ဖြတ်ကျော် စီမံဆောင်ရွက်ခြင်း (Data Storage and Cross-Border Processing)

CloudCityFood နှင့် ယင်း၏ နည်းပညာ ဝန်ဆောင်မှု ပံ့ပိုးသူများ၏ စနစ် တပ်ဆင်မှု အခြေအနေအပေါ် မူတည်၍ သင်၏ အချက်အလက်များကို မြန်မာနိုင်ငံအတွင်း သိမ်းဆည်း စီမံဆောင်ရွက်နိုင်သကဲ့သို့၊ အခြား နိုင်ငံ သို့မဟုတ် ဒေသများတွင် တည်ရှိသော တာဝန်ပေးအပ်ခြင်းခံရသည့် ဝန်ဆောင်မှု ပံ့ပိုးသူများမှ Cloud Hosting၊ မြေပုံ၊ တည်နေရာသတ်မှတ်ရေး၊ နည်းပညာ ထောက်ပံ့ရေး သို့မဟုတ် အခြား စီမံဆောင်ရွက်မှု ဝန်ဆောင်မှုများကို ပံ့ပိုးပေးနိုင်ပါသည်။ နယ်စပ်ဖြတ်ကျော် စီမံဆောင်ရွက်မှု ပါဝင်ပါက CloudCityFood သည် အောက်ပါ သင့်တော်သော အရေးယူဆောင်ရွက်ချက်များကို ကျင့်သုံးမည် ဖြစ်သည်

  • လက်ခံသူ၏ အချက်အလက် စီမံဆောင်ရွက်မှု ရည်ရွယ်ချက် နှင့် နယ်ပယ်ကို ကန့်သတ်ခြင်း၊ လက်ခံသူအား လျှို့ဝှက်ထိန်းသိမ်းမှု နှင့် လုံခြုံရေး တာဝန်များကို ထမ်းဆောင်ရန် တောင်းဆိုခြင်း၊ ခွင့်ပြုချက်မရှိဘဲ ရယူသုံးစွဲခြင်း၊ အသုံးပြုခြင်း နှင့် ထပ်မံ ထုတ်ဖော်ခြင်းများကို ကန့်သတ်ခြင်း၊ ဒေတာ၏ သဘာဝအပေါ် မူတည်၍ သင့်တော်သော လုံခြုံရေး အကာအကွယ်ပေးမှု အရေးယူဆောင်ရွက်ချက်များကို ကျင့်သုံးခြင်း။ သက်ဆိုင်ရာ နယ်စပ်ဖြတ်ကျော် စီမံဆောင်ရွက်မှုအတွက် သီးခြား သဘောတူညီချက် ရယူရန် လိုအပ်ပါက သင့်တော်သော နည်းလမ်းမှတစ်ဆင့် သင့်အား ရှင်းလင်းဖော်ပြမည် ဖြစ်သည်

အချက်အလက် လုံခြုံရေး (Information Security)

CloudCityFood သည် ပို့ဆောင်သူ အချက်အလက်များ၏ သဘာဝ၊ အထိအခိုက်မခံနိုင်မှု ပမာဏ နှင့် စီမံဆောင်ရွက်မှု ဘေးအန္တရာယ်များအပေါ် မူတည်၍ အချက်အလက်များကို ခွင့်ပြုချက်မရှိဘဲ ရယူသုံးစွဲခြင်း၊ အသုံးပြုခြင်း၊ ထုတ်ဖော်ခြင်း၊ ပြင်ဆင်ခြင်း၊ ပျောက်ဆုံးခြင်း သို့မဟုတ် ပျက်စီးခြင်းတို့မှ ကာကွယ်ရန် သင့်တော်သော နည်းပညာဆိုင်ရာ နှင့် အဖွဲ့အစည်းဆိုင်ရာ အရေးယူဆောင်ရွက်ချက်များကို ကျင့်သုံးမည် ဖြစ်သည်။ သက်ဆိုင်ရာ အရေးယူဆောင်ရွက်ချက်များတွင် အောက်ပါတို့ ပါဝင်နိုင်ပါသည်

  • ဒေတာ ပေးပို့ခြင်း နှင့် သိမ်းဆည်းခြင်းဆိုင်ရာ လုံခြုံရေး အရေးယူဆောင်ရွက်ချက်များ၊ အကောင့် မည်သူမည်ဝါဖြစ်ကြောင်း အတည်ပြုခြင်း နှင့် ခွင့်ပြုချက် စီမံခန့်ခွဲခြင်း၊ ဝန်ထမ်းများ နှင့် မိတ်ဖက်များ၏ ရယူကြည့်ရှုခွင့် ကန့်သတ်ချက်များ၊ ရယူကြည့်ရှုမှု အမှတ်အသားများ၊ လုပ်ဆောင်ချက် အမှတ်အသားများ နှင့် လုံခြုံရေး စောင့်ကြည့်မှု၊ ဒေတာ ကျောထောက်နောက်ခံ (Backup) ထားရှိခြင်း နှင့် ပြန်လည်ရယူခြင်း အရေးယူဆောင်ရွက်ချက်များ၊ စက်ပစ္စည်း၊ အကောင့် နှင့် ပုံမှန်မဟုတ်သော အကောင့်ဝင်ရောက်မှု ထောက်လှမ်းခြင်း၊ လုံခြုံရေး ဖြစ်စဉ် စုံစမ်းစစ်ဆေးခြင်း နှင့် ထိန်းသိမ်းဖြေရှင်းခြင်း လုပ်ထုံးလုပ်နည်းများ။ တိကျသော တည်နေရာ နှင့် ပို့ဆောင်ရေး လမ်းကြောင်းများသည် ပို့ဆောင်သူ၏ အခွင့်အရေးများအပေါ် အရေးပါသော လွှမ်းမိုးမှုရှိသည့် အချက်အလက်များ ဖြစ်ကြသည်။ CloudCityFood သည် သက်ဆိုင်ရာ အချက်အလက်များကို ကြည့်ရှုသုံးစွဲခွင့် အခွင့်အာဏာကို ကန့်သတ်ထားမည်ဖြစ်ပြီး အော်ဒါ ခွဲဝေခြင်း၊ ပို့ဆောင်မှု နောက်ယောင်ခံလိုက်ခြင်း၊ ဝင်ငွေ စာရင်းရှင်းလင်းခြင်း၊ လုံခြုံရေး စီမံခန့်ခွဲခြင်း၊ မတော်တဆမှု စုံစမ်းစစ်ဆေးခြင်း၊ ငြင်းခုံမှု ထိန်းသိမ်းဖြေရှင်းခြင်း သို့မဟုတ် သက်ဆိုင်ရာ တာဝန်များ လိုက်နာခြင်းတို့အတွက် လိုအပ်သော ကာလအတွင်း၌သာ သိမ်းဆည်းထားမည် ဖြစ်သည်။ အင်တာနက် ပေးပို့ခြင်း နှင့် အီလက်ထရွန်နစ် သိမ်းဆည်းခြင်းတို့သည် လုံးဝဥဿုံ လုံခြုံရေးကို အာမခံနိုင်ခြင်း မရှိပါ။ ပို့ဆောင်သူ၏ အခွင့်အရေးများကို အရေးပါစွာ ထိခိုက်စေနိုင်သော အချက်အလက် လုံခြုံရေး ဖြစ်စဉ် ဖြစ်ပွားပါက ဖြစ်စဉ်၏ သဘာဝအပေါ် မူတည်၍ သင့်တော်သော ထိန်းချုပ်မှု နှင့် ကုစားမှု အရေးယူဆောင်ရွက်ချက်များကို ကျင့်သုံးမည်ဖြစ်ပြီး သင့်တော်သော အခြေအနေတွင် ထိခိုက်ခံရသော ပို့ဆောင်သူထံ အကြောင်းကြားမည် ဖြစ်သည်

ဒေတာ ထိန်းသိမ်းသည့် ကာလ (Data Retention Period)

ကျွန်ုပ်တို့သည် ဤမူဝါဒတွင် ဖော်ပြထားသော ရည်ရွယ်ချက်များကို ပြည့်ဝစေရန် လိုအပ်သည့် ကာလအတွင်း၌သာ ပို့ဆောင်သူ အချက်အလက်များကို သိမ်းဆည်းထားမည် ဖြစ်သည်။ ယေဘုယျ ထိန်းသိမ်းသည့် မူဓမ္မများမှာ အောက်ပါအတိုင်း ဖြစ်သည်

  • အကောင့် နှင့် မည်သူမည်ဝါဖြစ်ကြောင်း အချက်အလက်များ
  • ပို့ဆောင်သူ အကောင့် တည်ရှိနေစဉ်၊ အရည်အချင်း စစ်ဆေးနေစဉ် နှင့် ဝန်ဆောင်မှု ပံ့ပိုးပေးနေစဉ် ကာလအတွင်း သိမ်းဆည်းထားမည်၊ မောင်းနှင်မှု နှင့် ယာဉ်ဆိုင်ရာ အချက်အလက်များ
